How I work with AI

Most of my work right now is building AI agents and automations, both at Right Click and at Opus Technologies, the company I co-founded. I use AI tools every day, but I review everything they write.

The reason for this is that AI still makes mistakes. What separates a good developer from a mediocre one is being able to catch those mistakes and fix them, instead of treating the AI as a source of truth.

AI is a tool, not a god.
